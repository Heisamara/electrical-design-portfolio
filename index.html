03 / BIM WORKFLOW

Data Preparation

PROPOSED WORKFLOW

A structured path from electrical drawing blocks to device inventories, validation reports, and Revit family mapping.

EZDXF · PANDAS · REVIT WORKFLOW
Proposed extraction fields

Block name, device ID, X/Y coordinates, layer, circuit ID, and equipment attributes.

Validation checkpoints
  • Duplicate equipment identifiers
  • Missing circuits or attributes
  • Unsupported electrical symbols

Outputs would include equipment and coordinate CSVs plus a family mapping template. Native Revit model creation requires a separate Revit/API workflow.
